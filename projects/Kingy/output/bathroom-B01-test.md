Which accessibility requirements apply inside these units: whether Livable Housing Design provisions apply in NSW for this approval, any seniors housing (NSW Housing SEPP) accessibility conditions in the consent, and the Uniting Design Guidelines (UDG) the sheet calls up. 2. Door: inward-opening, hinged leaf. Check against any requirement for sanitary compartment doors (open outward, slide, be removable, or have enough clearance to the pan) once the governing clause is in the library. Measured pan-to-doorway ≈ 1720.
3. Shower threshold: the "20" dimensions at the screen suggest a 20 mm element. Step-free entry can't be confirmed without a set-down section.
4. Grabrails: not provided; plywood noggings shown for future grabrails at the shower and WC.
